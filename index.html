About

Thoresen Media AB is an independent Swedish company that creates original entertainment: games, films and novels. The company was founded in 2021 and is based in Sweden.

We are a small team with a background in large game productions. We build our own worlds and stories, and we own and publish what we make.

What we do

Games

We design, develop and publish our own games. Our current production is a role-playing game for mobile devices.

Films

We develop original stories for the screen, from concept and script to visual development.

Novels

We write original fiction, including stories set in the worlds of our games and films.

In development

A role-playing game for iPhone and iPad

Our current project is a single-player role-playing game for mobile devices. It is in full development. The title will be announced later.
